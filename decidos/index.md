## Prototype

In order to use our prototype, you need [Yivi (identity wallet)](https://yivi.app/en/), which you can download from the 
- iOS 
  - [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/nl/app/yivi/id1294092994)
- Android
  - [Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.irmacard.cardemu)
  - [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/en/packages/org.irmacard.cardemu/)

Then, you need to **turn on Developer mode**, by:
1. pressing ‘More’ in the bottom bar and then 
2. pressing the version at the bottom quickly, seven times 

[Try the demo](https://decidos.fmjansen.com){: .button}
